Global Regulatory Status

The availability, approval status, and regulatory classification of Curespec products vary significantly by country and region. This page provides general guidance on the regulatory frameworks applicable to our offerings in different markets.

Users and healthcare professionals should verify the specific regulatory status and approved indications for each product in their respective country before use. Regulatory requirements continue to evolve, and this information should be reviewed periodically for updates.

02

Product Clearance & Approval

Our products have received regulatory clearance or approval in specific markets following evaluation by the relevant national authorities. The scope of approved uses, indications, and contraindications may vary between markets based on submitted clinical data and regulatory requirements.

In markets where products are not yet approved, we continue to engage with regulatory authorities to pursue necessary clearances. Users should not use products outside their country of approval unless expressly permitted by local law.

03

Regional Variations

Different regions maintain distinct regulatory frameworks that affect how products are classified, approved, and used. These variations reflect regional differences in healthcare systems, clinical evidence standards, and public health priorities.

Products approved in one region may not be approved for the same use in another region. Healthcare professionals must understand the local regulatory status before recommending or using any product. See our Curespec Global page for current clinical experience by country.

04

Healthcare Professional Requirements

The regulatory status of our products determines whether they can be distributed to the general public, require a healthcare professional's prescription or oversight, or are restricted to specific clinical settings.

In jurisdictions where healthcare professional oversight is required, only qualified practitioners with appropriate credentials and training should prescribe or recommend our products. Requirements vary by country.

05

Local Regulations & Restrictions

Users are responsible for understanding and complying with all applicable local, regional, and national laws regarding the purchase, possession, and use of our products. Regulations may restrict distribution, use, or promotional activities.

For comprehensive information about regulatory status in your specific location, please contact your local healthcare authority or our team. This page is provided for informational purposes and does not constitute legal advice.
