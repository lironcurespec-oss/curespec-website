01 / Nephrospec™

Transforming Kidney & Hypertension Care

Nephrospec™ is a non-invasive Electro-Hydraulic Acoustic Therapy (eHAT) device designed to treat chronic kidney disease (CKD) and hypertension.

Non-invasiveNo anesthesiaNo hospitalizationNo radiation

Our patented eHAT technology is designed to:

01Stimulate the formation of new blood vessels

Enhancing microvascular function and reducing high blood pressure.

02Revitalize kidney health

By restoring damaged tissue and delaying or even preventing the need for dialysis.

03Enhance patients’ quality of life

Delivering measurable results within weeks.

04Reduce healthcare costs

By up to 80%, making treatment both accessible and efficient.
